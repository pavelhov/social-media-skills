# WATCH — an opening that earns its payoff

Brief and identity rules govern captions, music, CTA, loops, runtime and production consent. None of
these are universal requirements. WATCH is a writing method, not evidence of audience performance.

## W — Win the opening

Choose an observable event that earns curiosity, tension or recognition. Prepare 3–5 real alternative
openers: first frame, performed action, words (or none), sound, duration and bridge into the body. Change
the staging or entry point as well as the language. Keep every alternative true to the same payoff.
Use **hook-writer** for comparison. When text is forbidden, use action/composition and sound as allowed;
a muted-comprehension check must not silently change a spoken-dialogue format.

## A — Advance causally

Map desire → action → consequence → payoff. Review the payoff board and late cast before opening
motion. Let a cold reader explain the chain from the boards without the author's pitch; record the
actual answer or mark that review pending. If the action does not cause the consequence, revise it.

Each shot needs initial state → dominant action → completed end state. Choose cuts for a reason:
coverage, viewpoint, space, time or an intentional transformation. A continuous physical action needs
time and evidence of completion; a generic “cut every few seconds” rule must not sever it.

## T — Time action and sound

Budget anticipation, contact, movement, completion and reaction. Time natural dialogue, pauses and
speaker changes separately, then align compatible overlaps. Dialogue that requires a character to
finish swallowing, turning or crossing a threshold cannot borrow that action's time. Extend runtime,
add motivated coverage, or simplify the approved premise rather than speed through required beats.

## C — Cash the promise

Plan the visible payoff, its required cast and its named speaker. Check cast identity, action completion,
speaker/source, possession and payoff as critical requirements; keep unknowns unknown. No budget or
review-round limit makes a failed board ready for motion. An incorrect still requires a revised still
and matching review, not a longer negative motion prompt.

## H — Hold the ending

Give the audience time to read what changed and the response it produces. A resolved standalone ending
needs no CTA, loop or sequel hook. Where requested, a continuation hook opens a new question after
paying off this one. Route platform adaptation to **reels-script**, **tiktok-script** or **youtube-shorts**.
