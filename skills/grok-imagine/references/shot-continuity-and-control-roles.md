# Shot continuity and control roles

Use after the identity rules, causal script and executable hook are settled. This is a portable
planning method, not a provider API schema. The canonical integration owner is
`tools/integrations/grok-imagine.md` in the social-media-skills source checkout. Installed skill
collections may omit that repository directory; this continuity method does not require that file to
be locally present. Verify exact supported controls, route/version, mode combinations and receipts
against the installed authorized adapter, consulting the canonical guide when available. Do not
duplicate a capability matrix here, substitute an API route for an approved subscription CLI route,
or infer CLI support from API documentation.

## Review the ending before spending on the opening

The project package must establish desire → action → consequence → visible payoff. Review the actual
payoff board, all required late cast and the exact payoff speaker before any opening motion. Ask a
cold reader to reconstruct the causal chain from the ordered boards without the author's explanation.
Record the answer, reviewer, story revision, assets/hashes, named predicates and uncertainty. No actual
review means pending, not pass. Critical cast identity, completed action, speaker/source, possession
and payoff cannot be downgraded to cosmetic to make the package fit a budget or review-round limit.

Reject a still with the wrong doctor, an extra critical creature, contradictory contact geometry or a
missing required actor before motion. Negative prompt wording cannot repair a wrong still. Correct and
review the still under the applicable image authorization; do not animate it in hope of a better cast.
A required actor is explicit, never “if the doctor is present.” Scope cast, props and body rules to each
shot; do not paste irrelevant absent-character constraints into every prompt.

## Give each shot a local contract

- **Initial state:** who is where, body pose, opening/obstacle size, contact and prop possession.
- **Dominant action:** a chronological performance with enough time for anticipation, contact,
  movement, completion and readable reaction. Preserve expressive acting and physical comedy.
- **Completed end state:** observable proof of completion. Traversal means the whole required body
  and carried prop have cleared the threshold, not that entry has begun or reached mid-entry.
- **Cast/speakers:** named visible actors and required offscreen sources; exact lines and speaker turns.
- **Invariants:** identity, body count/anatomy, prop count/shape/owner, relevant direction and clothing.
- **Allowed transformations:** named transformation_id, exactly which subject/property may change,
  when/how, and what persists.
- **Timing and coverage:** action windows, natural dialogue, compatible overlaps, frame/move and cut reason.

Do not demand large motion while freezing the actor. State the intended movement and the invariant
that actually matters (identity, possession, completed contact), rather than suppressing performance.
If the action and dialogue do not fit, revise timing/coverage within the brief; never silently alter
dialogue or necessary action. Runtime comes from the script, clip count from beats and verified limits.

Plan both local start and completed-end boards for review. A reviewed end board is planning evidence;
endpoint pinning is an execution control, required only when the approved method calls for it. Do not
impose a universal endpoint-pin requirement or claim an unsubmitted board was sent to the provider.

## Separate the purpose of every input

| Input/evidence role | Purpose | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Identity reference | The approved character, prop or style design | Exact start pose, endpoint, or preceding action success |
| Start composition | Local frame-one layout, cast, contacts and geometry | Completion of the requested action |
| Local end target | The same shot's desired completed state, if supported | The preceding action path or the next location's establishment |
| Timed keyframe | An intended intermediate state at a supported time | Reliable motion between states without review |
| Observed outgoing evidence | Selected upstream attempt/output hash plus actual outgoing frame and action review | A future shot's success, or continuity after replacing the upstream selection |

Keep role bindings explicit even if the same file serves two justified roles; retain its content hash
for each binding. Check the authorized adapter supports the requested combination and records what
was actually submitted. Required unsupported or missing controls block launch; do not silently drop
them. Prompt-only description must never be reported as a submitted endpoint or identity control.

The last sampled frame may help establish outgoing pose, but completed traversal and dialogue need
appropriate temporal/synchronized evidence. A desired storyboard cannot stand in for observed output.
Bind dependent inputs to the selected attempt. Changed bytes or selection invalidate dependent review.

## Design the edit, not an accidental morph

- `continuous`: preserve the same action/world state, direction, cast and possession; use matching
  selected outgoing evidence for the dependent start.
- `hard_cut`: deliberately change viewpoint, location or time, naming what persists. Complete the
  necessary preceding action, then establish the new space in its own shot. Do not interpolate spaces.
- `match_cut`: name the matching shape/pose/movement and the intended world/time change. This is an
  edit; visual similarity does not establish continuous travel.
- `transformation`: name the intentional transformation_id and specify the authorized change and unchanged invariants; review the
  transformation separately from accidental mutation. It may need its own shot and explicit cut plan.

**Entry example:** an exterior shot ends with both feet, torso and parcel beyond the doorway and an
empty threshold held long enough to read. A hard cut then establishes the interior with the same actor
and parcel. The interior board is its own starting composition, not the exterior shot's pinned end.
Review the selected entry action before proceeding to the dependent interior.

## Review and consent stay separate

Approval covers the disclosed first-pass scope; a spending ceiling does not permit corrective rerolls.
Follow exact repair-batch consent, preserve successful shots, and reconcile uncertain jobs before retry.
Transport success, technical export, visual quality, audio quality, story quality and release status
are separate. Review the selected master with complete synchronized playback; contact sheets and ASR
alone cannot prove exact dialogue, correct speakers, timing or music absence. A critical fail/unknown
remains a draft at any budget or review-round count. Offline contract tests do not establish live
visual reliability or audience performance.
